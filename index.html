Our Story

A home in the heart of the valley

Nestled in Barangang village in the breathtaking Mechukha valley of West Siang, Orchid Homestay offers an authentic retreat into one of Northeast India's most pristine landscapes.

Surrounded by the mighty Himalayas, lush forests, and the flowing Siyom river, we offer a rare combination of natural beauty and genuine Adi hospitality — a true home away from home.

Whether you're a trekker, a nature lover, or simply seeking peace away from the noise of the world, Orchid Homestay welcomes you with open arms.
